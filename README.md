## Deep-Dive Chosen: Customer Segmentation
A business-rule/RFM-lite approach is used:
- **Recency:** days since the customer's most recent order.
- **Frequency:** number of distinct orders.
- **Monetary:** total sales generated by the customer.
- Each metric is scored from 1–5 and combined into an RFM score.
- Customers are grouped into At Risk, Needs Attention, Potential, Loyal and Champions.

### Important data limitation
There are 947 unique customers across 1000 orders, so most customers appear only once. Therefore Frequency has limited variation. Present this as **RFM-lite / customer-value segmentation**, not as a long-term retention or churn model.

## Key Findings
1. **Electronics** is the highest-revenue category with ₹50,778,581.70.
2. **Laptop** is the highest-revenue product with ₹25,443,008.51.
3. **Patna** is the highest-revenue city with ₹19,285,966.89.
4. The strongest sales month is **2025-03**, with ₹13,059,899.94.
5. The dashboard focuses on customer segments plus category, city and monthly sales filters.
